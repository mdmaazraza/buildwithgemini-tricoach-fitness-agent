# My agent: tricoach-fitness-agent
One-liner: A conversational fitness coach that helps athletes log workouts, compute training zones, and generate motivational visuals.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Workout history, personal fitness preferences, target race dates, and heart rate thresholds
- Tools: Log workout, calculate training zones, fetch workout logs
- Catalog/UI: Exercise logs and weekly training schedule rendered as cards and tables
- Image gen: Motivational workout progress and race day visuals
- Sandbox: Pace, power, and training stress score calculations

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch: code sandbox for training metrics calculations
